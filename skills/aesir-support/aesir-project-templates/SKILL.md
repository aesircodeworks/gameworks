# Project templates

Load a template with `skill_view('aesir-project-templates', file_path='templates/<name>.md')` and copy into the **game workspace** after path approval.

Registries live under `templates/registries/`. Collaborative protocols live under `templates/collaborative-protocols/`.

Do not write templates into `$HERMES_HOME`.
