Do not suggest post-cutoff APIs until you have read, for the selected engine:

1. `references/<engine>/VERSION.md`
2. `references/<engine>/breaking-changes.md`
3. The relevant `references/<engine>/modules/` or `plugins/` file

Index: `references/README.md`.
